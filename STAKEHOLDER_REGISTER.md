## Key Insights & Patterns

### Immediate Priorities (Manage Closely)
1. **Legal/Compliance Officer** — Only negative attitude marker (`- !`); critical for regulatory and fairness risk mitigation. Early evidence building required.
2. **Finance Director** — High power, high interest, neutral. Business case clarity needed to move toward champion.
3. **Operations Manager** — High power, high interest, neutral. Phased rollout conversation critical.

### Support Network (Current Champions)
- Executive Sponsor, IT/Data Owner, Customer Success Lead, Pilot Customers (if available)
- These 4 stakeholders form natural advocate group; leverage for credibility with skeptical stakeholders

### Risk Areas
- **Fairness & Compliance:** Legal/Compliance Officer (already flagged `!`). Recommend legal review of algorithm and pricing principles before customer communication.
- **Job Security:** HR/Change Lead and Pricing Team Lead concern area. Recommend clear retraining and role evolution communication.
- **Organizational Change:** Sales and HR managing team resistance. Communication cadence and change story critical.
