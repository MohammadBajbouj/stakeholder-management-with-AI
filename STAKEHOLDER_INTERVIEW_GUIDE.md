## Core Interview Questions (30-45 minutes)

### **Section A: Role & Context** (5 minutes)

1. **Can you describe your role and how you interact with car rental operations?**
   - *Listen for:* Authority level, scope of influence, decision-making power

2. **How does pricing currently impact your work or decisions?**
   - *Listen for:* Pain points, priorities, constraints

---

### **Section B: Stakeholder Interests & Priorities** (10 minutes)

3. **What are your main priorities for this AI pricing project?**
   - Note power level (directive vs. advisory)
   - Note interest level (high engagement vs. monitoring)

4. **What outcomes would make this AI implementation successful from your perspective?**
   - *Listen for:* Alignment with organizational goals vs. individual goals

5. **What concerns do you have about implementing AI pricing?**
   - *Listen for:* Risks, compliance, fairness, operational challenges

---

### **Section C: Power & Influence** (8 minutes)

6. **Who else needs to approve or support this decision?**
   - *Map:* Decision chain, veto players, champions

7. **What could derail this initiative from your viewpoint?**
   - *Listen for:* Hidden constraints, political dynamics, resource dependencies

8. **Are there any non-negotiable requirements or constraints from your department/stakeholder group?**
   - *Listen for:* Hard boundaries, compliance rules, budget limits

---

### **Section D: Attitude & Support** (8 minutes)

9. **How do you feel about using AI to make dynamic pricing decisions?**
   - *Assess attitude:* Supportive (+) / Neutral (0) / Skeptical (-)
   - *Note risk:* Does this topic trigger urgent concerns (!)

10. **What would need to happen for you to champion this initiative with your peers?**
   - *Listen for:* Conditions for support, influence path, peer concerns

11. **What information or evidence would help convince you and your stakeholder group?**
   - *Listen for:* Communication strategy, proof points, transparency needs

---

### **Section E: Engagement & Next Steps** (8 minutes)

12. **How would you prefer to stay informed about this project's progress?**
   - *Map engagement strategy:* Manage closely / Keep satisfied / Keep informed / Monitor

13. **Who in your network should we definitely talk to about this?**
   - *Build stakeholder map:* allies, influencers, potential resisters

14. **What's the best way for our team to support you during this transition?**
   - *Identify support needs:* training, communication, involvement level

## Conflict Identification & Resolution (Issue #10)

### During Interviews, Watch for Conflicts:

**Pricing Philosophy:**
- Operations: "Lower prices to maximize volume"
- Finance: "Higher margins to maintain profitability"
- *AI Role:* Dynamic pricing balances both through data

**Transparency vs. Simplicity:**
- Customers: "Explain how price is calculated"
- Operations: "Keep algorithm proprietary to avoid gaming"
- *Resolution:* Communicate principle (demand/supply) without algorithm details

**Speed vs. Control:**
- AI Advocates: "Let algorithm optimize in real-time"
- Risk/Compliance: "Manual review before major changes"
- *Resolution:* Phased rollout with human-in-loop for high-impact decisions

**Adoption vs. Job Security:**
- Employees: "Will this automate my job?"
- Management: "We need faster, more consistent pricing"
- *Resolution:* Retraining plan, role evolution, performance incentives

## Briefing Synthesis Template (Issue #11)

### Three-Minute Briefing Elements

**[30 seconds] The What:**
- AI will determine optimal car rental prices based on demand, competitor pricing, and fleet availability
- System learns from historical data and adjusts recommendations daily

**[30 seconds] The Why:**
- Current manual pricing misses market opportunities
- Dynamic pricing competitors gain 15-20% revenue advantage
- AI ensures fair pricing while maximizing utilization

**[30 seconds] The How:**
- Algorithm: [1-sentence explanation of logic, not technical details]
- Rollout: Phase 1 (recommendations), Phase 2 (semi-automated), Phase 3 (full automation)
- Human oversight maintained at each phase

**[30 seconds] The Commitment:**
- Transparent decision-making: We document and explain major pricing changes
- Employee support: Training and role transition support for affected teams
- Customer fairness: Pricing follows clear principles; no discrimination

## Key Interview Tips

### What to Do ✓
- **Listen actively** — take notes, don't argue
- **Ask "why"** — dig into underlying concerns, not just surface complaints
- **Be specific** — reference actual business scenarios, not abstract concepts
- **Acknowledge constraints** — show you understand their real pressures
- **Follow up** — if they flag a concern, tell them when/how you'll address it
- **Build trust** — share what AI can and cannot do; be honest about uncertainty

### What to Avoid ✗
- **Don't oversell** — "AI will solve everything" loses credibility instantly
- **Don't dismiss concerns** — resistance often points to real risks
- **Don't interview in silos** — connect stakeholders so they see you're balancing interests
- **Don't make promises you can't keep** — better to say "I'll find out" than guess
- **Don't assume agreement** — silence ≠ support

## After All Interviews: Synthesis Steps

### Step 1: Data Collection (1 day)
- [ ] Review all interview notes
- [ ] Highlight quotes on power, interest, attitudes, concerns
- [ ] Note who referred other stakeholders

### Step 2: Pattern Recognition (1 day)
- [ ] What themes appear across 3+ interviews? (clusters of concern/support)
- [ ] What conflicts emerge? (what does Party A want vs. Party B?)
- [ ] What information gaps exist? (what do people not know?)

### Step 3: Mapping (1 day)
- [ ] Plot each stakeholder on power-interest matrix
- [ ] Add attitude markers (+, 0, -, !)
- [ ] Identify champions and potential blockers

### Step 4: Strategy (1 day)
- [ ] For each quadrant, draft engagement approach
- [ ] For each identified conflict, propose resolution
- [ ] Prepare stakeholder-specific briefing variations

### Step 5: Briefing & Feedback (2 days)
- [ ] Present 3-minute briefing to executive sponsor
- [ ] Share synthesis with core team
- [ ] Schedule follow-ups with key resisters
- [ ] Update engagement plan based on feedback
